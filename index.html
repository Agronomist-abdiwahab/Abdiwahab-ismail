"My work is grounded in one conviction: that Somaliland's prosperity is rooted in the soil. Every seed planted, every farmer trained, and every hectare restored brings us closer to a food-secure, green, and thriving nation."

Abdiwahab Ismail Abdi is one of Somaliland's most respected agricultural professionals. A holder of a BSc in Agriculture from the University of Hargeisa, he has dedicated over 12 years to the Ministry of Agriculture, rising to serve as Director of Plant Cultivation and Landscape Development — a role in which he shapes national agricultural strategy, leads large-scale field programs, and oversees the country's plant nursery and urban greening agenda.

His work spans the full spectrum of agricultural development: from hands-on fieldwork in remote farming regions to high-level policy development, inter-agency coordination, and engagement with international organisations including FAO, IFAD, and the World Bank. He has directly mentored hundreds of farmers across Burco, Ceerigaabo, Borama, Gabiley and beyond.

Abdiwahab is driven by the belief that sustainable agriculture is the foundation of Somaliland's economic independence — and that combining modern agronomic science with locally-grounded knowledge is the most effective path to food security and environmental resilience across the Horn of Africa.
