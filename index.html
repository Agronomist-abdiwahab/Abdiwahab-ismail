“My work is grounded in one conviction: Somaliland's prosperity grows from healthy soil, resilient farmers, and sustainable land use.”

Abdiwahab Ismail Abdi is an agricultural professional working in Somaliland's public sector with experience spanning agronomy, field extension, crop development, nursery systems, landscape development, disaster-risk management, food resilience, and programme coordination.

His portfolio combines practical field delivery with strategic coordination: farmer training, irrigation development, policy support, land restoration, urban greening, and stakeholder engagement with public institutions and development partners.

This website presents his career background, technical areas of focus, selected projects, publications, and contact information for collaboration, consultancy, and professional enquiries.
